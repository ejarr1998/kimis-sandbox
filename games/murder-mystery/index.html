How it's worked
The brief
Leave blank and the division assigns a random case file.
The division's files skew old-school — pick an era to force the case modern (or any decade you like).
⚰ Cast the victim (optional)
Name the person who dies. Everything else — the suspects, the motives, the whole case — gets built around them.
🎭 Pre-cast suspects (optional)
Give the writer up to 6 people to build the case around — a name and their role (their connection to the victim). Any of them can still turn out to be the killer. Blank rows are ignored.
